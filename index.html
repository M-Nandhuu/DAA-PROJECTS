About the Algorithms

1. Bubble Sort

Compares adjacent numbers and swaps them when they are in the wrong order.

2. Selection Sort

Finds the smallest number in the unsorted part and places it in the correct position.

3. Insertion Sort

Takes one number at a time and inserts it into its correct position in the sorted part.
